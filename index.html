Hi! I'm Gouri.

I'm Gouripriya S. Menon, an aspiring astrobiologist from Kerala, India. With a background in geology and palaeontology, I'm driven by a deep curiosity about the origins of life on Earth and how that understanding can guide our search for life elsewhere in our solar system and beyond.

I am a PhD student at the University of Copenhagen, Denmark, where I work on improving how we tell biological carbon apart from non-biological carbon in Earth's oldest rocks: the 3.7 to 3.8 billion-year-old Isua Supracrustal Belt in Greenland.

I've always been fascinated by the intersections of mythology, philosophy and science, and this space is where I hope to bring together my thoughts, experiences and scientific journey.
